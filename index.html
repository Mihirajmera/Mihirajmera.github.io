// 01 · OPERATING PRINCIPLES

What I believe about assurance

01

Compliance should compile.

Controls are code: versioned, tested and run continuously, not screenshotted once a quarter.

02

If it can't be re-performed, it isn't evidence.

Hashed, timestamped, traceable to the raw output and exportable as OSCAL.

03

A score is an opinion until someone checks it.

Scanners and red-team detectors are evidence sources, not verdicts. Keep the trail so a reviewer can verify.

04

AI agents are identities.

They hold tokens, shells and databases. Review their access like any privileged user's.
